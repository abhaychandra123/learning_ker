\chapter{Glossary}

\begin{longtable}{>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{3.6cm}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{11.4cm}}
\toprule
Term & Meaning \\
\midrule
\endhead
Adjoint & The transpose of an operator. For a convolution it is the 180-degree rotated filter \\
Amendment & A dated addition to a protocol, written before the number it affects is read (Chapter~\ref{ch:fista_depth}) \\
Amplitude (of an output) & $\langle y,t\rangle/\langle t,t\rangle$: how strong the output is along the target; 1 is right (Chapter~\ref{ch:ten_results}) \\
Atom & One small image in a dictionary, $g_m$ \\
BatchNorm & A layer that normalises each channel. It uses batch statistics in training and stored running statistics at test time \\
Binning & Averaging $f\times f$ pixels into one. Run 2 and run A use $f=2$ (0.2\um), run B uses $f=7$ (0.7\um) \\
Checkpoint & A saved copy of a model. Also a separate memory trick in \code{torch.utils.checkpoint} \\
Code & A map $x_m$ saying where and how strongly to stamp atom $m$ \\
COMSOL & The finite-element simulation we use as test data \\
Cosine similarity & $\langle y,t\rangle/(\lVert y\rVert\,\lVert t\rVert)$: how alike two patterns are, whatever their strength \\
Data-consistency step & One Tikhonov correction $X+\frac{H}{H^2+\mu}(B-HX)$ that makes the output agree with the measurement in the measured band. Our addition (Chapter~\ref{ch:consistency}) \\
Density & Fraction of code values that are not exactly zero \\
Dictionary & The set of atoms \\
Effective standoff & The height a source appears to be at. It includes conductor thickness, NV-layer thickness and optical blur \\
Epoch & One pass over the training data. Here, 600 crops; for MAGIC-UNet, 153,600 crops \\
Equivalent sheet current & $J_\text{eff}=J\,e^{-kd}$: the current at the reference plane that gives the same field as the true current a depth $d$ below it \\
FISTA & ISTA plus momentum \\
Height read-out, $h^\ast$ & The height at which the network's own current best explains the measured field (Chapter~\ref{ch:height}) \\
Ill-posed & A problem where the data alone does not fix the answer \\
ISTA & Gradient step plus shrink, repeated \\
$k_0$ & The extra decay constant in COMSOL's field, about 0.10\,rad/\um. A numerical property of the solve, not physics (Chapter~\ref{ch:comsol}) \\
$k_c$ & The frequency where the blurred signal sinks below the noise; the top of the measured band \\
$k_n$ & The frequency above which the COMSOL target is mostly noise: 2.9\,rad/\um \\
Lasso & Least squares plus an $\ell_1$ penalty \\
LISTA & ISTA unrolled into layers with learned weights \\
Lost-band floor & The error left if every frequency above the signal-to-noise crossing is lost and nothing is invented \\
MAGIC-UNet & The U-Net of Reed et al.\ 2025, from vector field to current \\
Mask & An array of 0s and 1s that hides the padding \\
Measured band & Frequencies below $k_c$, where the measurement still carries the signal (Chapter~\ref{ch:limits}) \\
Noise band & Frequencies above $k_n$, where the COMSOL target itself is mesh noise \\
NRMSE & Root mean square error divided by the target's range \\
Oracle & A score from a method fitted on its own test data: a ceiling, not a usable method \\
Pipeline floor & MAGIC-UNet's error with a perfect current, from cutting the image into tiles: 2.9\% \\
Plain & Eq.~20 with no threshold floor \\
Prior band & Frequencies from $k_c$ to $k_n$: real field the measurement has lost, which only a prior can fill in \\
Protocol & A file that fixes data, grids and decision rules before any score is read, with its hash recorded \\
Relative $L_2$ & $\lVert y-t\rVert_2/\lVert t\rVert_2$, our main score \\
Reproduction gate & A check that an old model still reproduces its old scores before anything new is computed with it \\
Residual & The mismatch between predicted and measured data \\
Run A, run B & The two 10\um\ MAGIC-UNet runs: 0.2\um\ pixels (12.8\um\ tile) and 0.7\um\ pixels (44.8\um\ tile) \\
Seed & The number that fixes a training run's random choices: starting weights, batch order, noise \\
Shrink, soft threshold & Move a value toward zero by $\theta$, stopping at zero \\
Sparse (run) & Eq.~20 with a floor on the thresholds, our addition \\
Speckle & Fine mesh noise in COMSOL fields \\
Standoff & Distance between source and sensor \\
Standoff ambiguity & A source at depth $h$ and a sharper one at $h+\delta$ give the same field \\
Stream & Training on a newly simulated layout for every crop \\
Strict & Plain Eq.~20 with MSE, at 32 and 64 layers \\
Stride & The step between neighbouring tiles; smaller than the tile, so tiles overlap \\
Tile & A $64\times64$ piece of the image that MAGIC-UNet processes at a time \\
U-Net & A network that shrinks the image in an encoder and grows it back in a decoder, with skip connections between them \\
Unrolling & Turning iterations into network layers \\
Upward continuation & The exact law for how a field changes with height \\
Wiener & $h/(h^2+\mu)$, a one-parameter deblurring filter \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\section*{This booklet}

\begin{itemize}

\item \code{booklet/make_figures.py} redraws the figures. \code{booklet/export_magic.py} exports the MAGIC-UNet data it needs from the server.

\end{itemize}
